# Caption & Voice Templates — copy, fill, post

> Templates built from her observed caption *structure*. All example wording is original placeholder text in that structure — fill every [BRACKET] with your own films and your own reasons. Do not copy her captions.

## Voice rules (pin this above your desk)
- First person, lowercase in English captions. `i` not `I`. Designed slides are capitalised; captions are not.
- Personal first, database second, question last. Always.
- Name every director. Never post a title without `Dir.`
- Pre-empt the “you missed X” comment in the caption, briefly and with humour.
- 5–6 hashtags, discovery tags only. No hashtag stuffing.
- One CTA only, matching the slide end card.
- Spanish subjects/partners: write the whole post in natural Spanish, don't translate your English draft.

## Pre-post voice check
- [ ] Does sentence 1 sound like a person, not a press release?
- [ ] Is there a personal reason before any list starts?
- [ ] Is every director named?
- [ ] Did you explain your most obvious omission before the comments do?
- [ ] Is there exactly one question and one CTA?
- [ ] Would this caption still work with the design hidden? (If not, the slides are carrying too much database — move dates/streaming into the caption.)
