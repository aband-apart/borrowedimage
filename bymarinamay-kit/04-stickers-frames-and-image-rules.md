# Stickers, Frames & Image Rules

## Sticker library — build these once, reuse forever
Make each sticker as a separate transparent PNG/SVG in Nunito ExtraBold, ALL CAPS, 20–24 units on a 1080 canvas. Slight rotation (-6° to +6°, random per sticker) is part of the look. Never align stickers to a grid.

### Genre stickers (Upcoming slides only)
ADVENTURE · THRILLER · SCI-FI · CRIME · ROMANCE · HORROR · DRAMA · ACTION · FANTASY · HISTORICAL · COMEDY · MYSTERY

### Judgement stickers
FAVE DIRECTOR / DIRECTOR FAV (add a tiny director's-chair icon) · BOOK ADAPTATION (book icon) · SEQUEL · DEBUT

### Mood stickers (Pt. 30 redesign set — pink/purple only)
HYPED · CAN'T WAIT ★ · UM… · PART [##] (thin dashed circle)

|---|---|---|---|
| Classic (Pt. 7 era) | Multicolour: red, mint #A8D8B9, mustard #E9B949, teal #2A9D8F, sky #A8C8E8, orange, purple, pink | Mixed: oval, rectangle, triangle, arrow, circular stamp, heart | Early Upcoming energy, busy and playful |

Placement rule: 3–5 stickers per film slide, overlapping the panel/photo edge, never covering the title or a face. One sticker may break onto the ground colour.

## Image treatment — step-by-step

### Halftone (her signature)
Photoshop / Photopea:
1. Image → Mode → Grayscale. Boost contrast until blacks are true black.
2. Image → Mode → Bitmap → Method: Halftone Screen. Frequency 12–18 lines/inch at final size, angle 45°, shape: Round. (For screen-only work, Filter → Pixelate → Color Halftone, radius 6–10, all channels 45, is the fast version.)
3. Back to RGB. Cut out the subject (Select Subject / remove background *before* halftoning gives cleaner edges).
Canva: search Elements for “halftone” overlays/duotone effects, or use the Duotone app on a B/W photo, then increase Grain.
Procreate/Affinity: halftone brush/pattern layer + clipping mask works for covers.

### Duotone into the ground colour
1. Start B/W and high-contrast.
2. Gradient Map: shadows → the ground colour's dark version (e.g. navy #2B3A55), highlights → cream or the accent (e.g. red #C8102E face on navy; orange on orange for Greek).
3. Check: the photo should feel *printed in* the ground, not pasted on it.

### Sepia classical head (franchise covers)
B/W photo → warm sepia gradient map (shadows brown, highlights cream) → halftone lightly → cut out → centre it, let stickers overlap the head. This head is a cover device only — don't use it on film slides.

### Grain (mandatory, last step)
Place `assets/grain-overlay-tile-540x675.png` on top of the finished, flattened slide, scaled to 200% so it covers the full 1080×1350, at 100% opacity (the tile is already transparent and subtle). Or: Photoshop Filter → Noise → Add Noise 12–18%, Gaussian, Monochromatic, on a 50% grey overlay layer at 30–50% opacity. Flat colour without grain reads as a different brand.

### Full-colour exception
FrameXFrame/art posts ONLY: keep the still in full colour (the film's colour is the evidence), add grain, thin black border. Duotoning an art-analysis still destroys the point of the post.

## Highlight covers (profile)
Build as circular badges: flat colour disc + one simple black icon/word in the centre + tiny lettering around the rim. Colours observed: pink (film journal, starburst), rainbow gradient (queer stuff, black heart), cream (art x cinema, frame motif), pale blue/teal (bookclub, stacked books), mustard (highlights, crown). Labels in lowercase under each circle.
